## Historical measurement caveat

The 24-hour host comparison summarized above is historical evidence, not a current live metric. The comparison included multiple changes and a CPU-steal shift; it cannot establish single-change causation. Kuma resource observations belong to an earlier/other interval unless explicitly identified. No traffic totals or per-job bandwidth/RAM measurements are present in the source repository. The learning simulators therefore label their outputs **[E]**.
